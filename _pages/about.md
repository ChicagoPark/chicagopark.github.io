I am a third-year Ph.D. student at [UW--Madison](https://engineering.wisc.edu/departments/electrical-computer-engineering/), fortunate to work with [Prof. Ulugbek S. Kamilov](https://ukmlv.github.io/). I was an AI research intern at [GE HealthCare](https://www.gehealthcare.com/en-us) in the summer of 2026, and a research intern at [Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://www.lanl.gov/) in the summers of 2024 and 2025. I graduated summa cum laude with a B.S. in Computer Science at WashU in 2024. My research interests lie in multimodal, multitask generative models and their applications to medical and real-world problems.
